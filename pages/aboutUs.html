About Code Everest

Learn to code,
one step at a time

Code Everest helps you learn programming on your own. You get a plan made for you, lessons and practice at every step, and an AI tutor who is there when you get stuck.

Why we built it

Starting to code can feel like a lot

Many beginners want to learn programming but do not know where to begin or how to keep going. Code Everest is made to help with exactly that.

explore

Too many places to start

There are endless tutorials, and it is hard to know which one fits you. Code Everest gives you one clear path to follow.

help

Not sure where you stand

It is easy to skip ahead or repeat things you already know. A short placement test suggests the right starting level for you.

support_agent

Stuck with no one to ask

When your code does not work, giving up is easy. The AI tutor explains ideas and errors and gives hints while you learn.

trending_up

Hard to keep going

Progress is easier to keep up when you can see it. Your roadmap shows where you are, what you finished and what comes next.

How you learn

Your climb, step by step

Every learner follows the same simple route, with a plan that fits their own level and schedule.

  1. 01

    Choose your path

    Pick the programming path you want to learn.

  2. 02

    Take a short placement test

    The test estimates what you already know, so you start at the right level.

  3. 03

    Get your roadmap

    See your plan as a mountain trail built around your study days. Each flag is a stage, and the next one opens when you finish the current one.

  4. 04

    Learn and practice

    Read lessons, take quizzes and solve coding challenges, with the AI tutor close by.

  5. 05

    Beat the Boss Fight

    Each unit ends with a Boss Fight, a bigger challenge or small project that shows what you have learned.

  6. 06

    Earn your certificate

    Finish a learning path and you can get a certificate to show your work.

Your AI tutor

Help that teaches, not just answers

The tutor is built to help you learn, so it guides you instead of doing the work for you.

  • check_circleAnswers programming questions and explains ideas in simple words
  • check_circleGives hints without showing the full solution
  • check_circleExplains error messages and gives feedback on your code
  • check_circleTells you when it cannot answer reliably

The AI tutor is here to help you learn. It is not a guarantee, so double-check important answers with your lessons.
